WORLDSMITH PRO: HOW TO INSTALL
==============================

Thank you for buying Worldsmith Pro!

1. Unzip this file. You'll get a folder called "worldsmith-pro" with three files inside:
   main.js, manifest.json and styles.css.

2. Put the "worldsmith-pro" folder into your vault's plugins folder:
       <your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/worldsmith-pro/
   (".obsidian" is hidden on some computers. In Obsidian, go to Settings > Community plugins
   and click the folder icon next to "Installed plugins" to open the plugins folder.)

3. In Obsidian: Settings > Community plugins. Click the refresh icon, then switch on
   "Worldsmith Pro".

That's it. Your worlds are kept in your vault, so everything you made with the free edition is
already there. If the free "Worldsmith" plugin is also switched on, Pro switches it off for you;
you can uninstall the free one.

UPDATES
Worldsmith Pro doesn't update itself. When a new version comes out, download it again and
replace the three files in the "worldsmith-pro" folder, then restart Obsidian.
